# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its community Code of Conduct.

Everyone who takes part in this project is expected to follow that covenant.
This applies to the repository, the issue tracker, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, and any other place where a person represents the project. We want
a community that is free of harassment and welcomes everyone.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately through
the repository's
[private reporting channel](https://github.com/brian-a-au/cja_auto_sdr/security/advisories/new).
Do not post a conduct report in a public issue or pull request.

The maintainer is responsible for explaining and enforcing these standards. The
maintainer will review each report fairly and promptly, and will protect the
privacy of the people involved as far as possible. The maintainer may remove or
edit contributions. The maintainer may also issue a correction, a warning, a
temporary ban, or a permanent ban, following the community impact guidelines in
the Contributor Covenant.

Anyone who handles a report must disclose a conflict of interest. If they cannot
act fairly, they must step aside from that report. A reporter or an affected
person may ask for a decision to be reconsidered through the same private
channel.
